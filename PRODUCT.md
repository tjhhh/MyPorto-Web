## Product Purpose

Serve as the definitive digital monograph and living portfolio for Muhammad Fauzan. Demonstrate authentic full-stack and mobile systems capability, architectural discipline, and high visual standards—converting skepticism into immediate conviction within 10 seconds of interaction.

## Positioning

Unlike template portfolios that recite generic tech tags and AI-generated copy, this portfolio functions as an editorial engineered artifact: rigorous systems thinking (distributed PostgreSQL schemas, real-time vehicle telemetry, provincial conservation systems) rendered through high-contrast editorial craft, physical digital materials, and an editorial architectural operator portrait.

## Operating Context

Visited on desktop workstations, retina laptops, and mobile devices by technical evaluators who scan rapidly and dismiss generic portfolios. Requires instant visual gravity, frictionless navigation, deep Detail drill-downs, and uncompromising 60fps performance.

## Product Principles

1. **Authorial Discipline over Generic Slop**: Every pixel, label, hairline, and typographic choice must feel authored with intent, never assembled from prefabricated components.
2. **Proof Over Posturing**: Present problems, architectures, database schemas, and trade-offs rather than buzzword lists.
3. **Physical Materiality in Digital Space**: 3D and motion must feel grounded in real physical qualities—lacquer, obsidian, warm gold reflection, and inertial weight.
4. **Editorial Tension and Structural Rhythm**: Asymmetry, generous whitespace, tight metadata, and monumental type that commands attention.
5. **Zero-Lag Craft Floor**: 60fps rendering, instant layout shifts, lazy-loaded WebGL, responsive from 360px mobile to 4K ultra-wide, and WCAG AA contrast compliance.
